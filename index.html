About Me

Software · Signal Processing · Acoustic Sensing

I am a Senior Software Engineer and Researcher with over 15 years of combined academic research and industry experience in software-hardware integration, sonar and acoustic sensing, data science, acoustic metamaterials, and robotics. I design experimental and computational systems that integrate sensing, signal processing, and machine learning. In my current role, I develop LabVIEW control, measurement, and calibration software and FPGA firmware in VHDL. One calibration utility I developed reduced calibration time by approximately 90%.

Previously, as a Data Science Fellow at the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), I built Python, PostgreSQL, and SAS data workflows that supported knowledge discovery from research articles.

My Ph.D. at the University of Maryland focused on acoustic metamaterials and bio-inspired sonar. That work included real-time sonar DSP on a TI C2000 microcontroller and a MATLAB machine learning pipeline for classifying echoes.

I have a strong background in fiber-optic sensing, 3D-printed metamaterial structures, FPGA-based systems, and scientific programming in Python, MATLAB, and C. I am also active in mentoring, teaching, and peer review, with publications in Applied Physics Letters and the ASME IMECE conference proceedings.

Research & Publications

Doctoral research · University of Maryland

Doctoral research · University of Maryland

High wave vector acoustic metamaterials and bio-inspired sonar

My doctoral work studied acoustic metamaterials that support wave propagation with high wave vector values, and used their energy confinement and radiation enhancement to improve acoustic emission and reception.

  • A low-dimensional fiber optic probe for measuring fields inside deep-subwavelength metamaterial cavities.
  • Metamaterial receivers with passive signal amplification, frequency filtering, and active tuning.
  • A metamaterial-enhanced ultrasonic emitter, with FEM parametric studies for design guidelines.
  • A combined metamaterial sonar on a robotic platform that tracked targets markedly better than a conventional sonar.
